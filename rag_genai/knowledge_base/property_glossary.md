# Property glossary

## BHK

BHK stands for Bedroom, Hall, Kitchen. A 3 BHK home has three bedrooms, a hall
(living room) and a kitchen.

## Carpet, built-up and super built-up area

Carpet area is the usable floor area inside the walls. Built-up area adds the
thickness of internal and external walls and areas such as balconies. Super
built-up area also includes a share of common areas like lobbies and
staircases. EstateIQ's model uses built-up area.

## Price per square foot

Price per square foot is the price divided by the area. It makes properties of
different sizes easier to compare.

## Property types

- Apartment: a unit in a multi-storey residential building or society.
- Builder Floor: an independent floor in a low-rise building, typically with
  one unit per floor.
- Villa: a standalone house, often in a gated community.
- Plot: a piece of residential land.

## Property status

- Ready to Move: construction is complete and the property can be occupied.
- Under Construction: the project is still being built.
- New Launch: a project that has recently been announced or opened for booking.

## Furnishing

Unfurnished properties have no furniture or fittings beyond basics.
Semi-Furnished properties include some fittings such as wardrobes, lights or a
modular kitchen. Fully Furnished properties are ready to live in with
furniture and appliances.

## Gated community

A residential project with a controlled entry, boundary wall and usually
shared amenities and security.
