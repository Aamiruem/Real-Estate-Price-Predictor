# Using EstateIQ

## Getting a price estimate

Sign in, open Predict Price and fill in the form. Required fields are city,
locality, property type, status, built-up area, bedrooms, bathrooms,
furnishing, facing, water supply and amenities. Optional fields (floor, total
floors, property age, parking, balconies, facilities and distances) make the
estimate more specific. Select Get valuation to see the estimated market
value, the price per square foot and a summary of the property.

## Choosing a locality

Pick a city first; the locality list then shows only the localities the model
was trained on for that city.

## Understanding the result

The estimated market value is the model's prediction in Indian rupees, also
shown in lakh or crore. Price per square foot divides the estimate by the
built-up area. The Model insights section lists the attributes with the
strongest influence on the model. Treat every result as an indicative estimate
and verify it with a local property professional before any transaction.

## Saving your requirements

The Requirements page stores a brief of your preferred city or area, property
type, budget range, bedrooms and bathrooms. The brief is saved only in your
browser. It is not sent to the prediction model and does not search listings.

## Contacting EstateIQ

Use the Contact page to send an enquiry about property valuation, technical
support or anything else. Enquiries are saved for the EstateIQ team.

## Accounts

Create an account with your name, email, phone number and a password of at
least 8 characters. Sessions use secure cookies and refresh automatically.
